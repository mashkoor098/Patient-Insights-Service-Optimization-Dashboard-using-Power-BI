# Healthcare Insights Dashboard with Power BI

## Project Overview

This project uses Power BI to analyze data from hospitals, focusing on 1,000 patients across 5 hospitals. The goal is to provide insights that can help hospitals improve patient care, reduce treatment costs, and optimize hospital operations. The dashboard highlights key areas like patient demographics, treatment outcomes, and hospital costs.

## Tools and Technologies

- **Power BI**: Used to create interactive dashboards and visualizations.
- **Data Analysis**: Analyzed hospital and patient data to uncover meaningful patterns.
- **Statistical Methods**: Applied statistical techniques to identify trends and correlations.
- **Healthcare Knowledge**: Interpreted the data using insights into healthcare processes and patient care.

## Dataset

The dataset used in this project comes from two Excel sheets and includes information on 1,000 patients from 5 hospitals. The data includes:

### Patient Information
- **Patient ID**: Unique identifier for each patient
- **Name**: Full name of the patient
- **Age**: Age at admission
- **Gender**: Gender of the patient
- **Blood Type**: Blood type
- **Diagnosis**: Primary diagnosis
- **Treatment**: Treatments administered
- **Admission Date**: Date of hospital admission
- **Discharge Date**: Date of discharge
- **Total Bill**: Total cost of hospital stay
- **Prescription Details**: Details of medications prescribed

### Hospital and Staff Information
- **Hospital**: Name of the hospital
- **Doctor Name**: Attending doctor
- **Room Number**: Patient's room number
- **Daily Cost**: Daily cost of the patient's stay
- **Treatment**: Treatments administered
- **Recovery Rating**: Patient's recovery rating after treatment

## Business Question

The main question this project seeks to answer is:  
**"How can hospitals reduce treatment costs while ensuring or improving patient recovery outcomes across different age groups and treatment types?"**

## Key Insights

### 1. Cost Analysis of Treatment Types
- **Observation**: Surgery has the highest average cost per patient.
- **Recommendation**: Consider using less expensive treatments like physical therapy or medication where possible, as these can reduce costs without sacrificing patient recovery.

### 2. Treatment Costs vs. Recovery Rating
- **Observation**: Surgery doesn’t always lead to the best recovery ratings, despite its high cost.
- **Recommendation**: Focus more on treatments like counseling, which offer high recovery ratings at a lower cost.

### 3. Age Group Analysis
- **Observation**: Recovery outcomes vary significantly across different age groups, with seniors showing the most variability.
- **Recommendation**: Develop age-specific treatment plans, especially for seniors, to improve outcomes and use resources efficiently.

### 4. Patient Admissions Trends
- **Observation**: Certain months see a higher number of patient admissions for specific diagnoses.
- **Recommendation**: Use admission trends to better plan and allocate resources, ensuring efficient hospital operations during peak times.

## Next Steps

### High Priority

1. **Optimize Treatment Plans for Better Recovery and Cost Efficiency**
   - **Action**: Focus on treatments that offer the best recovery outcomes at the lowest cost.
   - **Steps**: 
     - Prioritize cost-effective treatments like counseling and physical therapy over more expensive options like surgery.
     - Review current treatment protocols to identify high-cost, low-recovery treatments and replace them with more effective alternatives.

2. **Tailor Treatments for Seniors**
   - **Action**: Create age-specific treatment strategies for seniors to improve recovery rates.
   - **Steps**:
     - Analyze recovery data by age group to identify effective treatments for seniors.
     - Implement targeted interventions and adjust treatments to better match the needs of older patients.

### Medium Priority

3. **Leverage Admission Trends for Resource Management**
   - **Action**: Use patient admission data to manage hospital resources more effectively during peak times.
   - **Steps**:
     - Track monthly admission patterns and identify high-demand periods.
     - Adjust staffing levels, bed availability, and supply inventories to prepare for peak admissions.

### Low Priority

4. **Explore Alternatives to Surgery**
   - **Action**: Investigate less expensive treatments as alternatives to surgery.
   - **Steps**:
     - Pilot programs for alternative treatments like physical therapy for conditions usually treated with surgery.
     - Compare the outcomes and costs of these alternatives to the current methods and consider wider adoption if successful.
